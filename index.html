The Long Beak

I will use this page to test how my long nose can find answers when the dark feels like a wall.

I found a tiny insect all by myself, in the dark. My beak alone could reach it. I proved I can find answers even when no one is speaking.

Tonight, after the dance until my legs could not speak, I did not call out into the dark and waited. Something small ran through the leaves and was gone, leaving rings on the water. Old Tuck shut one eye and opened it again, saying: "Slowly, Old Tuck lets out a long breath. Nothing more. Just the quiet wind."

I watched him say he was still there, that the water sings loud, and that I should rest now. The silence did not feel big. It felt like being found when I do not know where to stand.
